Privacy & about

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What this site is

nowwhat is a one-page overview of what is happening in the world. It is refreshed every hour by an automated script. Headlines link to the original publishers; we do not republish articles.

AI-written summary and translations

The two-sentence summary at the top of the page is written automatically by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) from the headlines on the page. The Chinese, Spanish, Hindi and Arabic versions of the summary and headlines are also machine-translated by AI. Both can be incomplete or wrong. Always check the linked source stories.

What we collect

We do not have accounts, sign-ups or forms, and we do not collect your name or email. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, to count visits.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, and you can turn off personalized ads in My Ad Center. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Data sources and embedded widgets

News headlines come from publishers' public RSS feeds. The market map and ticker are embedded widgets provided by TradingView, which loads them from its own servers and may set its own cookies under TradingView's privacy policy. App charts, Wikipedia page views and prediction-market odds come from public sources. Data may be delayed. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

Local weather

The weather in the top corner is for your approximate city, worked out by Cloudflare from your IP address. We do not ask for your precise location and we do not store it. Forecasts come from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway / yr.no) under CC BY 4.0; we send them only a rounded location (about 10 km), never your IP address.

Your language choice

If you pick a language, your browser remembers it on your device (local storage). It is not sent to us.
