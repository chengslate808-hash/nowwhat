About nowwhat

nowwhat is the world on one page. Open it and in ten seconds you know the biggest news stories, how markets are moving, what people are downloading, reading and betting on — in English, 中文, Español, हिन्दी or العربية.

Frequently asked questions

What is nowwhat?
A free website that shows what is happening in the world right now on a single page: the top 5 world news stories, a 2-sentence AI summary, global stock market heatmaps, top apps, most-read Wikipedia articles and prediction-market odds.
How are the top 5 stories chosen?
nowwhat reads the public RSS feeds of 11 outlets — BBC, Al Jazeera, The New York Times, The Guardian, NPR, DW, CNBC, France 24, Sky News, CBS and ABC — and ranks stories by how many outlets cover the same story at the same time. Every headline links to the original publisher.
How often is it updated?
News headlines, prediction-market odds and the AI summary refresh every 30 seconds while people are on the site. App charts and Wikipedia trends refresh hourly. Market heatmaps come from TradingView and may be delayed.
Who writes the summary at the top?
An AI model (Anthropic's Claude) writes a neutral 2-sentence summary from the current headlines only, whenever the top story changes and at least once an hour. It can be wrong, so check the linked source stories.
Which languages are supported?
English, Chinese, Spanish, Hindi and Arabic — the five most-spoken languages in the world. Headlines and summaries are machine-translated: 中文 · Español · हिन्दी · العربية.
Is there an archive of past days?
Yes — the daily archive has one page per day (UTC) with that day's AI summaries and biggest stories, plus an RSS feed.
Is it free?
Yes. No sign-up. It may show ads.
